# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, background, gender identity, experience level, nationality,
appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming.
- Give and accept feedback graciously — reviews are about the code, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with non-native English speakers.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained unwelcome contact.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Soliciting funds, promoting scams, or asking contributors for private keys or seed phrases.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that
do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors
for behavior they judge inappropriate.

Report concerns to **ju@nimiq-ju.fr**. Reports are handled confidentially.
